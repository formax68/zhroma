Contract (the v1 preference queue is the model; copy its shape, not its literals): `admit` answers `rejected` at once, without reading or writing, when `registry.parseValue` rejects the value or `registry.encode` returns undefined; answers `failed` at once when queued plus active jobs reach `maxPending`; otherwise queues a job with a deadline timer of `timeoutMs`. One job is active at a time. The active job calls `read(key)`, which resolves `{ ok: true, present, raw }` or `{ ok: false }`; `ok: false` answers `failed` and never writes. It then resolves the current value; for a cas key a request revision different from the current revision answers `conflict` with the current revision and writes nothing; when the current status is not `unreadable` and `registry.equal` says the value is already in effect it answers `unchanged` and writes nothing (D-11); otherwise it encodes (cas revision = current + 1) and calls `write(key, stored)`, which resolves a boolean, answering `saved` or `failed`. An unreadable stored value is replaced by a save, because the agent chose a value (D-08; this plan's assumption A1). At the deadline a job that has not started is removed and answers `failed`; a job whose read is in flight answers `failed` and must not write afterwards; a job whose write is in flight answers `unknown`. The next job starts only after the active job's physical write promise has settled, even after its requester was answered.
