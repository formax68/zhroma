Contract (the v1 preference queue is the model; copy its shape, not its literals): `admit` answers `rejected` at once, without reading or writing, when `registry.parseValue` rejects the value or `registry.encode` returns undefined; answers `failed` at once when queued plus active jobs reach `maxPending`; otherwise queues a job with a deadline timer of `timeoutMs`. One job is active at a time. The active job calls `read(key)`, which resolves `{ ok: true, present, raw }` or `{ ok: false }`; `ok: false` answers `failed` and never writes. At the deadline a job that has not started is removed and answers `failed`; a job whose read is in flight answers `failed` and must not write afterwards; a job whose write is in flight answers `unknown`.
