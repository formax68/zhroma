Stop the final gate from authorizing `proceed` on evidence that has been
relabeled out of scope or replaced with arbitrary private strings.

Purpose: `01-VERIFICATION.md` truths 5 and 14 FAILED on CR-07 and CR-09. Final
mode requires only that each evidence ID be *present*: a probe changed

production, so replacing all three paint summaries with three distinct private
strings also returned `proceed`.

per-ID evidence contract that the final verdict is derived from.

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Ledger field values → verdict authorization | Scope, status, and scenario labels decide whether required evidence counts. |
| Recorded paint summaries → privacy boundary | A free-text paint field can carry tenant content into the repository. |
| Test-local validator → production gate | A strictness gap between them is a bypass with a passing test suite. |

## STRIDE Threat Register — ASVS Level 1, block on high

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-57 | Tampering | required-evidence labelling | high | mitigate | A per-ID contract fixes expected scope, admissible statuses, and admissible scenarios; because all fifteen required IDs record `scope: English path`, the already scope-gated localization exemption becomes unreachable by any of them once scope is contract-bound (CR-07). |
| T-01-58 | Information Disclosure | interaction paint fields | high | mitigate | Move the closed paint grammar into `scripts/interaction-evidence.js`, call it from production, canonicalize before distinctness, and reject without echoing values (CR-09). |
| T-01-59 | Spoofing | test-versus-production divergence | high | mitigate | One exported assessor, imported by both the final gate and the smoke suite; a `git grep` acceptance criterion proves single definition. |
| T-01-60 | Repudiation | verdict reproducibility | medium | mitigate | Blocker codes are emitted in one declared order, and transposing ledger sections must not change the result. |
| T-01-61 | Denial of Service | empty or blank ledger input | low | mitigate | Zero-entry and blank-required-field ledgers reject with the stable coded rejections introduced in Plan 01-09. |
| T-01-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | mitigate | No package install occurs; the pinned approved toolchain and the lockfile are untouched. |
</threat_model>
