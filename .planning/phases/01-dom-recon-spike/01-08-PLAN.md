## Trust Boundaries

| Manifest path → fixture bytes | Relative paths, symlinks, and checksums determine which files enter validation. |
| Fixture bytes → parser and scenario contract | Untrusted markup must be scanned, parsed inertly, and proven to match its named scenario. |
| Ledger + manifest → Phase 2 verdict | Evidence completeness and blocking decisions jointly control downstream implementation. |
| Authenticated-session history → provenance/security record | Human-only facts about origin and operational safety cannot be inferred from sanitized bytes. |
