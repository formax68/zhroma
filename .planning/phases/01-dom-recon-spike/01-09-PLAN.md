<assumption_delta_decision>
The detector fired `pluralization` on **fallback** in the ROADMAP goal line ("the
fallback if the answer is no"). Phase 1 does record more than one selector
fallback: paired Garden identifiers, then paired `data-test-id` selectors, then a
structural fallback that `SELECTORS.md:293` states is *not* proven.

- **Primary noun:** the *ranked fallback chain* — an ordered list of selector
  strategies each carrying its own proof state — not a single "the fallback".
- **Decision:** `promote`. The gate must read a machine-readable, per-rung proof
  state rather than the current single prose sentence. This is exactly CR-08's
  finding: a prose sentence describing a *future* obligation currently satisfies
  the authorization predicate. Plan 01-15 promotes the ranked chain to structured,
  validator-owned evidence and demotes the prose to human-readable commentary.
- **Rationale:** with one prose blob standing in for the whole chain, a rung that
  is admittedly unproven cannot be distinguished from a rung that passed.
- **Companion invariant (accepted, planned in 01-15):** a rung whose proof state
  is not `proven` can never contribute to authorization, for every rung.
</assumption_delta_decision>
